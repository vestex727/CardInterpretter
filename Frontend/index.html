Nexus of Cards

Welcome to the Nexus of Cards. A website that will allow you to translate your foreign language cards to English. Perfect for draft nights, or for your games of commander.
In the hamburger menu in the top left you will find pages for draft, hand, board, your graveyard, your opponnent's graveyard, history, and an about page.


The draft page is for during the drafting phase. With that in mind, draft mode only displays the translation for the cards in your pictures. Everytime a new picture is uploaded, the old ones are removed.

The hand page is used to display the translations of the cards in your hand. From this page, cards can be sent to the board, your graveyard, or removed.

The board page is meant for cards on the board, and is meant to be public information. Cards from the board can be sent to your hand, your grave, your opponent's grave, or removed.

Both your grave and your opponent's grave are self explainatory.

The History page holds all cards that have been scanned by the user. Cards can be sent to the other pages from here, and they can be permantly removed from here as well.
